# Licensing guidance review

**Status:** v0.1 draft (2026-10-17), Phase 10. Reviewer status for every row: **pending operator/legal**.
**Source of the claims:** `packages/contracts/src/guidance/licensing.ts` (TECH_DECISIONS D60). Every string in that file is listed below; nothing has been paraphrased away.
**Where the text is shown:** the `LicensingGuidance` component (`apps/web/src/components/guidance/licensing-guidance.tsx`) renders the category summary, the category items, then the general items, then the disclaimer. Items flagged `requiresConfirmation: true` carry the suffix "(confirm before travelling)" in the UI. It is mounted on three pages:

| Public vehicle page `/vehicles/[slug]`              | `apps/web/src/app/vehicles/[slug]/page.tsx` | full section          |

| Customer booking page `/bookings/[id]` (all states) | `apps/web/src/app/bookings/[id]/page.tsx`   | compact (collapsible) |

The category block is selected by the vehicle category id. `scooter` and `tuktuk` are inactive categories (`PROTECTED_INACTIVE_CATEGORY_IDS`), so their text is not reachable through a live listing today; it is still shipped and is reviewed below.

## Method and limits

- Verification date for this table: **2026-10-17**. Nothing below was verified against a primary source (Motor Traffic Act, Department of Motor Traffic publications, police circulars) during Phase 10; the external verification work of Phase 10 covered the Data Protection Act, PayHere and hosting vendors only. Every legal claim is therefore "not verified".
- "Decision" is the recommended handling for launch: **verified** (keep as is), **cautious** (keep, but with the wording recommended in the row or already present), **remove** (do not show until confirmed).
- This file does not invent driving requirements. Where no primary source is known, the row says "no primary source found".
- The recommended cautious wording everywhere is: "Confirm the current requirements before travelling."

## Findings and recommendations

1. **Summaries carry no flag.** The six `summary` strings are shown in bold above the items and are not marked `requiresConfirmation`, so they appear without the "(confirm before travelling)" suffix even though they state licence classes that have not been verified. Recommendation: either add the cautious wording to each summary ("...; confirm the current requirements before travelling.") or render the suffix for summaries as well. This is a content change in `licensing.ts` and must be made by the owner of that file (LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED before removing the hedge instead).
2. **`helmet` / `scooter-helmet` were unflagged legal claims** (resolved 2026-10-18: both items now carry `requiresConfirmation: true`, so the UI adds "(confirm before travelling)"; the wording itself is unchanged pending a primary source). They state a legal obligation ("mandatory") with `requiresConfirmation: false` and no source in the repository. Recommendation: set `requiresConfirmation: true` until a primary source (Motor Traffic Act regulation or Department of Motor Traffic notice) is recorded here, or reword to "Wear a helmet; the provider will tell you what the law requires."
3. **`foreign-licence`** names two issuing bodies for the recognition permit. Keep the hedged wording; record the primary source here once counsel confirms it.
4. **`tuktuk-foreigners`** is correctly worded as a report, not a fact, and the category is inactive. No change; confirm with the Department of Motor Traffic before the category is ever activated (CLAUDE.md: scooter and tuk-tuk activation refused in code).
5. **No item states an age, engine-size threshold, probationary rule or insurance requirement as law.** Good; do not add any such rule without a source recorded in this file.
6. **Three languages.** The guidance is English only. SECURITY_AND_PRIVACY §7 plans Sinhala and Tamil for the privacy notice; the guidance should follow once the English text is approved (product decision, not Phase 10).
