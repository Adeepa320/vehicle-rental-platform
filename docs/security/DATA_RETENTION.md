# Data retention policy and inventory

**Status:** v0.1 draft (2026-10-17), Phase 10 (W8). Owner: founder until a privacy lead is named ([PLACEHOLDER: DPO_CONTACT]). Every period marked **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** is a proposal, not a decision.
**Related:** docs/SECURITY_AND_PRIVACY.md §6 (data inventory) and §8 (initial retention assumptions, which this document supersedes once approved), docs/TECH_DECISIONS.md D61 (documents), D64 (search logs), D65 (anonymisation), D69 (privacy requests), [../legal/PROCESSOR_REGISTER.md](../legal/PROCESSOR_REGISTER.md), the Privacy Notice (`packages/contracts/src/legal/content/privacy-notice.ts`, section "How long we keep data").
**Source of truth for what exists:** `packages/database/src/schema/*.ts`. Every table listed below exists there; tables from the design documents that were never built (`otp_codes`, `provider_documents`, `vehicle_documents`, `file_objects`, `ledger_entries`, `settlements`, `customer_driver_details`) are not listed.

## 1. Principles

1. **Keep what the service, the law and the money trail need; delete or anonymise the rest.** Account deletion is anonymisation (D65): personal fields are removed, history stays with its foreign keys.
2. **One writer per deletion.** Rows are removed only by the mechanisms named below (the planned `retention.sweep` job, the anonymisation transaction, cascades on the rows those two touch). Nobody deletes by hand in production.
3. **Legal holds beat retention.** A purge never touches a record that has an open booking, an open dispute, a refund due, an unresolved payment or an open privacy request, and never touches privileged accounts (section 4).
4. **Append-only stays append-only.** `booking_events`, `payment_events`, `audit_events` and `dispute_messages` are never updated; they are retained for the life of their parent or for the financial period, whichever is longer.
5. **Objects follow their rows.** A file in object storage is deleted only together with, or after, its database row, never before.
6. **Sweeps are idempotent and logged** (counts only; never the personal data removed).

## 4. Legal holds and blockers

A purge or anonymisation **must not** run for a record while any of the following is true. The anonymisation service enforces the account-level list today (`409 ACCOUNT_ANONYMISATION_BLOCKED` with `details[].issue`); the planned sweep must apply the record-level list.

| Condition                                                             | Scope                                                      | How it is detected                                                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Open booking (`requested`, `accepted`, `confirmed`, `active`)         | Account (as customer or provider owner); booking records   | `bookings.status` not final                                                                                                                                                     |
| Open dispute (`open`, `in_review`)                                    | Account (both parties); booking, documents of the provider | `disputes.status`                                                                                                                                                               |
| Refund due (`payments.refund_due_amount > 0`, `anomaly = refund_due`) | Account (customer); booking records                        | `payments`                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Unresolved payment (`payments.requires_manual_resolution = true`)     | Account (customer); booking records                        | `payments`                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Privileged account (`admin`, `super_admin`)                           | Account                                                    | `users.roles`                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Open privacy request (`open`, `in_review`)                            | Account                                                    | `privacy_requests`; completed by the operator, never purged while open                                                                                                          |
| Open security incident or legal matter naming the record              | Any                                                        | Manual: the incident log names the record ids and the hold end date; engineering pauses the relevant sweep step by setting the platform setting that the sweep reads (Phase 10) |

## 5. Data-subject deletion: what happens, step by step

1. The user submits a deletion request from the account page (`POST /users/me/delete-request`, Phase 10) or writes to the privacy contact; an operator may record it on their behalf (`privacy_requests.source = admin`).
2. The request is acknowledged (response-time rules in the Privacy Notice, LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED) and shows its current blockers.
3. The operator clears blockers by normal operations only: bookings complete or are cancelled through the state machine, disputes are resolved, refunds are recorded.
4. The operator completes the request, which runs `AdminUsersService.anonymise` with the typed e-mail confirmation (one transaction, audited `user.anonymised`), and the requester receives the courtesy e-mail at the old address.
5. Subsequent sweeps remove the remaining time-boxed data (sessions, tokens, documents of a closed provider when enabled, driver snapshot when enabled).
6. Backups age out according to section 3.7; if a backup is restored in the meantime, step 4 is replayed for every anonymisation after the backup time.
