# Runbook: support

**Status:** v0.1 (2026-10-17), Phase 10. Audience: whoever answers the support inbox (the founder during validation) and the on-call engineer.
**Related:** docs/OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md (every console procedure), [INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md](INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md), [DATA_BREACH_RESPONSE.md](DATA_BREACH_RESPONSE.md), [../security/DATA_RETENTION.md](../security/DATA_RETENTION.md), the legal drafts in `docs/legal/`.

## 2. Triage

1. Is money, safety or personal data at risk, or is the site down? → INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md (SEV1 / SEV2), reply to the user that it is being handled.
2. Is it a request about personal data (access, export, correction, deletion)? → §4.6.
3. Otherwise find the object: booking reference (`/admin/bookings` search), user e-mail (`/admin/users`), provider (`/admin/providers`), listing (`/admin/vehicles`). Read the timeline before replying.
4. Act only through the console routes below; never promise what the console cannot do (no manual confirmation, no automatic refund, no editing prices or timers).
5. Record the outcome in the ticket; where the console action takes a reason, write it as if the affected person will read it (several reasons are shown or e-mailed to them).

## 4. Paths by topic

### 4.1 Payments

| Situation                                 | Where to look                                                                                                         | Action                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "I paid but the booking is not confirmed" | `/admin/bookings/[id]` payment summary; `/admin/payments` for the attempt; API `GET /admin/payments/{id}` with events | If pending: **Check with gateway** (`POST /admin/payments/{id}/reconcile`; `503` when Merchant API credentials are absent, then compare with the merchant portal). Never confirm by hand. If PayHere shows success and the booking expired: it is a `late_success`, refund due (OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md §4.4)                                                                  |
| "Where is my refund?"                     | Payment row: `refund_due_amount`, `refunded_amount`, events `payment.refund_due` / `payment.refund_recorded`          | Explain the policy (docs/legal/CANCELLATION_POLICY_DRAFT.md): full refund due or forfeited, by actor and the 48 hour rule. If due and not yet recorded: process outside (portal or bank transfer; ask for bank details by e-mail, do not store them) then **Record refund** (`POST /admin/payments/{id}/refund`, mode `manual` with reference, or `gateway` when configured) |
| "I was charged twice"                     | Two `paid` rows for one booking show `duplicate_payment`                                                              | Record the refund of the duplicate (OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md §4.3); **Mark resolved** (`POST /admin/payments/{id}/resolve`) if already refunded externally                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Chargeback letter, bank error             | `payment_events` `chargeback` anomaly; portal                                                                         | Record what the console allows (resolve with a note), keep references, escalate to L2 (INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md §8.11)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

### 4.2 Providers

| Situation                                            | Where                                                                                                          | Action                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Application status question                          | `/admin/providers/applications/[id]`                                                                           | OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md §1.1: start review, request changes, approve, reject (`POST /admin/provider-applications/{id}/...`), each with a reason                                               |
| "My listing is not visible"                          | `/admin/vehicles/[id]`: status, photos (≥ `MIN_VEHICLE_PHOTOS`), active location and category, provider active | Explain the searchable predicate (approved listing, active provider, active location and category, enough photos, no hold on the dates). Review or reactivate via `/admin/vehicles` actions |
| Listing paused after document expiry                 | `/admin/documents`, `/admin/vehicles/[id]` audit `vehicle.auto_paused`                                         | Provider uploads the renewed document (`/provider/documents`); admin approves; admin reactivates the listing (`POST /admin/vehicles/{id}/reactivate`)                                       |
| Provider wants to cancel a confirmed booking         | `/provider/bookings/[id]` (provider does it) or `/admin/bookings/[id]` **Cancel** on behalf                    | Provider cancellation → full refund due by policy; then record the refund                                                                                                                   |
| Provider asks to change identity fields of a vehicle | `/admin/vehicles/[id]`                                                                                         | Identity fields are locked after approval (`VEHICLE_IDENTITY_FIELDS`); category correction via `POST /admin/vehicles/{id}/category`; anything else means a new listing                      |

### 4.3 Documents

| Situation                     | Where                                                        | Action                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Review queue                  | `/admin/documents` (pending first)                           | Open (`GET /admin/documents/{id}/content` streams; every view is audited `document.viewed`), then approve (optionally correct `expires_on`), reject with a reason, or request replacement (OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md §1.2, §2.2)  |
| Provider cannot upload        | API error code (`413` too large, `415` not PDF / JPEG / PNG) | Explain the limits (10 MB; PDF, JPEG or PNG only); the file is sniffed by content, so a renamed file is refused                                                                                                               |
| Suspicious or forged document | The document and the provider's others                       | INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md §8.10: reject neutrally, pause listings if there is a safety risk, internal notes, escalate to L2 and legal; do not tell the provider the suspicion                                                      |
| "Delete my document"          | `/admin/documents/[id]`                                      | There is no delete action. Explain the retention rule (DATA_RETENTION.md §3.5: rejected after 30 days, superseded after 90 days, current while active, LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED). Uploading a replacement supersedes the old one |

### 4.4 Disputes

| Situation               | Where                                                                                     | Action                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| New dispute             | `/admin/disputes?status=open`, `/admin/disputes/[id]`                                     | OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md §5: start review, internal note, public message, resolve (`no_action`, `provider_warning`, `customer_warning`, `refund_recommended`, `resolved_between_parties`, `other`) or reject, each with a public note |
| "Open a dispute for me" | Booking page of the party (`/bookings/[id]/dispute` or `/provider/bookings/[id]/dispute`) | Only a participant can open it, within 14 days (`dispute_window_days`); one per booking. Tell them where; do not open it on their behalf (no such admin route)                                                                     |
| Dispute asks for money  | `/admin/payments` for the booking                                                         | A decision never moves money; `refund_recommended` is a pointer. Record a refund only when due under the policy or decided by L2                                                                                                   |

### 4.5 Security

| Situation                                   | Action                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "Someone used my account"                   | Suspend (`/admin/users/[id]` → Suspend, reason) which signs out every session; ask the user to reset the password once reactivated; INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md §8.7                                                            |
| Admin lost authenticator and recovery codes | L3 runs `pnpm admin:mfa-reset --email <admin> --reason "<identity confirmed how, when>"` after confirming identity by a second channel; the admin re-enrols at next sign-in; `pnpm admin:mfa-report --strict` afterwards |
| Phishing e-mail quoting our brand           | Collect headers, warn users through a status note if widespread, check the sending domain's DMARC reports, escalate to L3                                                                                                |
| Report of a vulnerability                   | Thank, do not argue, forward to L3 the same day, record in the incident log; do not pay or promise bounties without L2                                                                                                   |
| Suspected data exposure                     | DATA_BREACH_RESPONSE.md immediately                                                                                                                                                                                      |

### 4.6 Privacy requests (access, export, correction, deletion)

| Request               | Self-service                                                                                                    | Operator path                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Deadline                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Access / export       | Account page download (`GET /users/me/export`, Phase 10; JSON `vrp-user-export/1`, lists capped at 1,000 items) | If the user cannot sign in: verify identity through the account e-mail, record the request (admin privacy-request creation, Phase 10 console queue), have L3 produce the export, hand it over by a secure channel, complete the request with a public note                                                        | Acknowledge at once; respond within one month, extendable by two months with notice before the first month ends; free of charge (Amendment Act No. 22 of 2025 s.4; Part II not yet in operation; LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED) |
| Correction            | `PATCH /users/me` from the account page (name, phone, country, language, currency)                              | Anything else (e-mail change, provider fields): L3 through a reviewed change; record it                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Same                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Deletion              | Account page (`POST /users/me/delete-request`, Phase 10, e-mail typed as confirmation)                          | `/admin/users/[id]` shows `anonymisation.allowed` and blockers (open booking, open dispute, unresolved payment, refund due, privileged role). Clear blockers by normal operations, then complete the request, which runs `POST /admin/users/{id}/anonymise` (reason + e-mail confirmation; irreversible; audited) | Same. Tell the user what blocks completion and that history is kept anonymised (Privacy Notice, retention section)                                                                                                      |
| Objection / complaint | Write to the privacy contact                                                                                    | L2 with counsel; record in the privacy request queue as an access-type note if no other type fits                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Same; inform the user of the right to complain to the Data Protection Authority (https://www.dpa.gov.lk/)                                                                                                               |

Never e-mail an export to an address other than the account e-mail. Never anonymise on a verbal request. Never anonymise an admin (remove the role first through engineering).

## 5. Template replies (adapt; never paste personal data of the other party)

- **Payment pending**: "Thank you. We can see your payment attempt for booking <reference>. The gateway has not yet confirmed it to us. We are checking with the gateway now and will update you today. Your reservation is held until <confirm_by in Sri Lanka time>; please do not pay again."
- **Refund due**: "Your booking <reference> was cancelled <by whom> on <date>, <n> hours before pickup. Under our Cancellation and Refund Policy the advance of LKR <amount> is refunded in full. We are processing it and will confirm by e-mail when it is recorded; card refunds can take 5 to 10 days to appear."
- **Forfeited**: "Your booking <reference> was cancelled on <date>, less than 48 hours before pickup. Under our Cancellation and Refund Policy the advance is not refundable in that case. If you believe this is wrong you can open a dispute from the booking page within 14 days."
- **Deletion request received**: "We have received your request to delete your account. We will complete it once <blockers> are closed, and confirm by e-mail. Booking, payment and audit records are kept in anonymised form as described in our Privacy Notice."

## 6. Reporting

Weekly: count of tickets by topic, open refunds due (`/admin/payments`), open disputes, open privacy requests and their age, incidents. Monthly: review this runbook against the tickets; propose console or copy changes through the normal process.
