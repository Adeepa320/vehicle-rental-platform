# Demo assets

Provenance of every externally sourced file used by the **local realistic demo** (`pnpm demo:seed`, README “Local realistic demo”). These assets are for local visual review only: they are never part of a production or staging image (`.dockerignore` excludes them and `apps/api/Dockerfile` removes `scripts/`) and the demo seed refuses non-local environments.

## Licence

All photos come from **Pexels** under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/): free to use and modify, attribution not required (credited here anyway). Not allowed under that licence, and not done here: selling unaltered copies, implying endorsement by people or brands shown, or redistributing the photos as a stock collection. The listings are fictional demo data; the photos show **similar vehicles, not the advertised vehicle**, and no business or person shown is associated with the platform. No Unsplash+ / premium, watermarked, scraped or hotlinked image is used; nothing is loaded from a third-party URL at runtime.

## Processing

- Downloaded once from the Pexels CDN (`images.pexels.com`, width 1600) on 2026-10-28, checked visually for model relevance.
- Stored in `apps/api/scripts/demo/photos` as JPEG re-encoded with sharp (longest side ≤ 1600 px, quality 80): all metadata (EXIF, GPS, ICC, XMP) is dropped.
- Legible number plates were blurred (Gaussian) before storage.
- `pnpm demo:seed` uploads each file through the API's own pipeline (`processVehiclePhoto`: byte sniffing, orientation, metadata stripped, WebP `thumb` / `medium` / `large` variants in the public bucket, original in the private bucket, server-generated keys).
- Model substitutions (exact-model photos were not available under a free licence): the demo lists the model the photos actually show (Corolla instead of Axio, Swift instead of Alto, RAV4 and Land Cruiser Prado instead of Raize, Honda CB350 RS instead of Yamaha FZ). The KDH van's three photos show three different vans of the same generation (HiAce H200).
- Rejected during sourcing: a set with a legible Sri Lankan-format plate on a real local car (Honda Civic, not used), mislabelled models (CR-V tagged HR-V, TownAce tagged HiAce, newer HiAce generation), images with people in frame or custom decals.
